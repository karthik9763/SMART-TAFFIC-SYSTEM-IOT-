About the Project

"Smart Traffic Density Prediction using IoT and Deep Neural Networks (DNN)" is a comprehensive B.E. Final-Year Computer Science & Engineering thesis project designed to mitigate traffic congestion, reduce emissions, and provide intelligent traffic light management telemetry.

Key Technical Highlights

Deep Neural Network (DNN) Model

Multi-layer Perceptron (MLP) architecture (64 -> 32 hidden units) trained on traffic count, velocity, lane occupancy ratio, and temporal hour features.

OpenCV Computer Vision Engine

Background subtraction (MOG2), noise filtration, contour detection, aspect ratio classification, and Region of Interest (ROI) monitoring line.

IoT ESP32-CAM Microcontroller

Captures live MJPEG video stream over local Wi-Fi, streaming directly into Flask backend for real-time video inference.

Full-Stack Flask & SQLite

8 relational tables, session security, role-based access controls, interactive Chart.js analytics, and automated PDF report export.
